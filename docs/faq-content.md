## 1. Face Cosmetic Surgery

**What facial cosmetic surgery do you offer?**
Our facial surgery is led by Consultant Plastic & Reconstructive Surgeon Mr George
Filobbos. Popular procedures include blepharoplasty (upper and lower eyelid surgery),
neck lift, and neck & chin liposuction. We are also expanding our range of facial
procedures, including treatments under general anaesthetic such as rhinoplasty.

**What is blepharoplasty (eyelid surgery)?**
Blepharoplasty is a surgical procedure that addresses both cosmetic and functional
concerns of the eyelids. It removes excess skin, muscle and fat from the upper and/or
lower eyelids to give a refreshed, more youthful and rested appearance, and can also
improve vision where excess upper-lid skin is a problem.

**Is eyelid surgery done under general anaesthetic?**
Blepharoplasty is performed under local anaesthetic, so you remain awake but the area
is fully numbed and you will not feel pain. This avoids the risks and longer recovery
associated with general anaesthesia and means you can go home the same day.

**How long is the recovery after eyelid surgery?**
We recommend taking 7–10 days off work, particularly if your role is public-facing, as
swelling and bruising are most noticeable during this time. You should avoid eye
make-up for at least 10 days, or until the incisions are fully healed. Most people feel
comfortable returning to normal activities within two weeks.

**Will I have visible scars?**
Incisions are carefully placed within the natural creases of the eyelids, so scars are
well concealed and typically fade significantly over the following months.

**What is a neck lift and who is it for?**
A neck lift tightens loose skin and refines the contour of the neck and jawline. It
suits people troubled by sagging skin, banding or a loss of definition along the jaw.
At consultation we will assess whether a neck lift, neck & chin liposuction, or a
combination will best achieve your goals.

**How do I know which facial procedure is right for me?**
The first step is a one-to-one consultation with our surgeon, who will examine the
area, discuss your goals and medical history, and recommend the most appropriate
option. There is no obligation to proceed, and you will be given clear, honest advice.

## 2. Breast Surgery

**What types of breast surgery do you offer?**
We offer a comprehensive range of breast surgery, including breast augmentation
(enlargement), breast uplift (mastopexy), combined breast augmentation & uplift, breast
reduction, combined breast reduction & uplift, breast re-augmentation (revision
surgery), and the removal or exchange of existing implants.

**Who performs breast surgery at Victoria House Clinic?**
All breast surgery is carried out by our GMC specialist-registered plastic surgeons,
led by Medical Director Mr George Filobbos, in our CQC-registered clinic.

**What is the difference between a breast augmentation and a breast uplift?**
A breast augmentation increases the size and improves the shape of the breasts, usually
with implants. A breast uplift (mastopexy) raises and reshapes breasts that have begun
to sag, without necessarily changing their size. Many patients choose to combine the
two for both added volume and a lifted, firmer shape.

**Can I have my old breast implants removed or replaced?**
Yes. We offer implant removal and exchange, as well as breast re-augmentation (revision
surgery) for patients who wish to change implant size or type, or who are experiencing
problems with existing implants. Your surgeon will assess your individual situation at
consultation.

**How do I choose the right implant size and shape?**
Implant choice is a careful, personalised decision. During your consultation your
surgeon will take measurements, discuss your goals and lifestyle, and help you choose a
size, shape and profile that gives a natural, proportionate result for your body.

**How long does recovery take after breast surgery?**
Recovery varies by procedure and individual, but most patients take around 1–2 weeks
off work and avoid strenuous exercise and heavy lifting for around 4–6 weeks. A
supportive surgical bra is usually worn during healing. Your surgeon and team will give
you detailed, personalised aftercare guidance.

**Will breast surgery affect breastfeeding or breast cancer screening?**
Many women are able to breastfeed after breast surgery, though it cannot be guaranteed.
Implants can affect how routine mammograms are performed, so it is important to tell
your screening provider that you have implants. We will discuss these considerations
fully with you before surgery.

## 3. Procedures for Men (Vasectomy, Circumcision, Dorsal Slit)

Our men's health procedures are carried out in our CQC-registered clinic and led by
Consultant Urological Surgeon Mr Hosam Serag, who specialises in vasectomy,
circumcision and dorsal slit procedures.

### Vasectomy

**What is a vasectomy?**
A vasectomy is a simple, highly effective form of permanent male contraception. The
surgeon makes a small incision on either side of the scrotum and blocks off the vas
deferens — the tubes that carry sperm — so that sperm can no longer reach the semen.

**Does a vasectomy hurt and how long does it take?**
The procedure is carried out under local anaesthetic, so the area is numbed and you
should not feel pain, only mild pressure. It is a straightforward procedure that
usually takes under an hour to perform.

**How much does a vasectomy cost?**
A vasectomy at Victoria House Clinic costs £750. This is a transparent, all-inclusive
price; full details are confirmed at your consultation.

**Is a vasectomy effective immediately?**
No. You are not protected straight away, and you must continue to use additional
contraception until a follow-up semen analysis confirms there are no sperm remaining.
This usually takes a number of months and several ejaculations to clear.

**Is a vasectomy reversible?**
A vasectomy should be considered a permanent form of contraception, and we do not offer
vasectomy reversal. You should only proceed if you are confident you do not want
children, or any more children, in the future. We will discuss this carefully with you
before booking.

**What is recovery like after a vasectomy?**
Most men recover quickly. Some mild swelling, bruising or aching is normal for a few
days and can be managed with supportive underwear, rest, ice and simple pain relief. We
provide detailed aftercare advice and recommend avoiding heavy lifting and vigorous
exercise for around a week.

### Circumcision

**What does an adult circumcision involve?**
Circumcision is the surgical removal of the foreskin from just behind the head of the
penis, using a scalpel or surgical scissors. It is a straightforward and quick
procedure carried out under local anaesthetic, so the area is numbed and you will not
feel pain during the procedure.

**Why might someone need a circumcision?**
Circumcision is performed for medical reasons (such as a tight foreskin/phimosis,
recurrent infections, or skin conditions) as well as for personal, cultural or
religious reasons. Your surgeon will discuss your reasons and suitability at
consultation.

**How much does circumcision cost?**
Adult circumcision at Victoria House Clinic starts from £1,750. The exact price is
confirmed at consultation.

**Can I go home the same day?**
Yes. After the procedure you will spend time in our onsite recovery room where you are
supported and monitored. Most patients are able to go home after a couple of hours'
rest — it is a same-day procedure.

**What is recovery like after circumcision?**
Some swelling and discomfort is normal in the first days and settles over a few weeks.
You will be given clear aftercare instructions, including wound care, hygiene and when
to resume exercise and sexual activity. Full healing typically takes several weeks.

### Dorsal Slit

**What is a dorsal slit procedure?**
A dorsal slit is a minor surgical procedure that relieves tightness around the
foreskin. The surgeon makes a small incision to divide the foreskin, leaving the head
of the penis partially exposed to relieve tightness and pain. It is often considered an
alternative to full circumcision where preserving the foreskin is preferred.

**How is a dorsal slit different from a circumcision?**
A circumcision removes the foreskin entirely, whereas a dorsal slit divides the
foreskin to release tightness while preserving it. A dorsal slit can be a good option
for men who want relief from a tight foreskin but wish to keep the foreskin.

**How long does the dorsal slit procedure take?**
The procedure is minor and typically takes around 30 minutes, performed under local
anaesthetic.

**How do I know which men's procedure is right for me?**
The best way is to book a consultation with our urologist, Mr Hosam Serag, who will
examine you, explain your options, and recommend the most suitable procedure for your
needs.

## 4. Body Cosmetic Surgery

**What body procedures do you offer?**
Our body cosmetic surgery includes mini tummy tuck and abdominoplasty, liposuction
(including neck and chin), fat transfer, and labiaplasty. We are also expanding our
range of body surgery performed under general anaesthetic.

**What is the difference between a mini tummy tuck and a full abdominoplasty?**
A mini tummy tuck addresses loose skin and a small amount of excess fat below the navel,
with a shorter scar and quicker recovery. A full abdominoplasty addresses the whole
abdomen — tightening muscle and removing more excess skin — and suits those with greater
laxity, often after significant weight loss or pregnancy. Your surgeon will advise which
is right for you.

**What is fat transfer?**
Fat transfer (fat grafting) removes unwanted fat from one area of the body using
liposuction, purifies it, and re-injects it to add volume and contour elsewhere. It uses
your own natural tissue, making it a popular option for soft, natural-looking
enhancement.

**What is labiaplasty and why do women choose it?**
Labiaplasty reshapes or reduces the labia. Women choose it for comfort (relief from
twisting, chafing or pain during exercise or intercourse), for hygiene, or for cosmetic
reasons. It is carried out by our consultant plastic surgeons, and at our clinic
procedures such as labiaplasty can be performed under local anaesthetic.

**Will I be awake during body surgery?**
Several of our procedures, including labiaplasty, are performed under local anaesthetic
so you remain awake but comfortable and pain-free. Larger procedures may require general
anaesthetic; your surgeon will explain the most appropriate option for you.

**How long is recovery after body cosmetic surgery?**
Recovery depends on the procedure. Minor treatments under local anaesthetic involve only
a short downtime, while a tummy tuck typically requires around 2 weeks off work and 4–6
weeks before resuming strenuous activity. You will receive detailed, personalised
aftercare guidance.

## 5. Laser Treatments

**What laser treatments do you offer?**
Our laser treatments are powered by Candela®, a world leader in laser aesthetics, and
include laser hair removal, laser skin rejuvenation, laser treatment for pigmentation,
and laser tattoo removal (including the removal of eyebrow tattoos and scalp
micropigmentation).

**Which laser machines do you use?**
We use the Candela® GentleMax Pro for laser hair removal and the Candela® PicoWay for
advanced skin rejuvenation, pigmentation and tattoo removal. These are premium,
clinically proven platforms.

**Is laser hair removal suitable for my skin type?**
Our GentleMax Pro delivers safe, effective and permanent hair reduction for a wide range
of skin types (Fitzpatrick types 1–5). A patch test and consultation are carried out
beforehand to confirm suitability and tailor your treatment.

**How many laser hair removal sessions will I need?**
Because hair grows in cycles, a course of several sessions spaced a few weeks apart is
needed to target hairs in their active growth phase. The exact number depends on the
area, your hair and skin, and is discussed at your consultation.

**What can laser skin rejuvenation treat?**
The Candela® PicoWay supports advanced skin rejuvenation that can reduce the appearance
of scars and fine lines, treat pigmentation such as sunspots, age spots and freckles,
and stimulate collagen production for fresher, more even-toned skin.

**How does laser tattoo removal work?**
The PicoWay laser delivers ultra-short pulses of energy that break tattoo ink into tiny
particles, which the body then naturally clears. It can treat body tattoos, eyebrow
tattoos and scalp micropigmentation. Multiple sessions are usually required, depending
on the size, age, colour and depth of the tattoo.

**Is there any downtime after laser treatments?**
Most laser treatments involve little to no downtime. Some temporary redness or mild
sensitivity is normal and settles quickly. We provide aftercare advice, including sun
protection, to help you get the best results.

**Do you offer any laser discounts?**
We run time-limited offers on laser treatments from time to time, and we offer a
discount for clients with PCOS. Please check our offers page or contact the clinic for
current promotions.

## 6. Skin Treatments

**What skin treatments do you offer?**
We offer a full range of medical-grade skin treatments, including anti-wrinkle
(muscle-relaxing) injections, dermal fillers, skin boosters, Profhilo and
polynucleotides, medical microneedling, chemical peels, medical facials, and Dermalux®
LED light therapy.

**Who carries out your injectable and skin treatments?**
All injectable and skin treatments are carried out by nurse prescribers or Level 4+
qualified practitioners, within our CQC-registered clinic, so you are in safe,
experienced hands.

**What is the difference between anti-wrinkle injections and dermal fillers?**
Anti-wrinkle (muscle-relaxing) injections soften lines caused by repeated facial
movement, such as frown lines and crow's feet. Dermal fillers restore lost volume and
smooth deeper lines, and can enhance features such as the lips and cheeks. They are
often used together for a balanced, natural result.

**What are Profhilo and polynucleotides?**
These are advanced skin-boosting treatments that hydrate the skin and stimulate collagen
and elastin from within, improving skin quality, firmness and radiance rather than simply
adding volume. They are an excellent option for overall skin rejuvenation.

**What is medical microneedling and what does it treat?**
Medical microneedling creates tiny controlled channels in the skin to trigger the body's
natural healing and collagen production. It can improve skin texture, fine lines,
enlarged pores, and the appearance of scarring, including acne scarring.

**What does a chemical peel do?**
A chemical peel exfoliates the surface layers of the skin to reveal smoother, brighter,
more even-toned skin underneath. Peels can help with dullness, pigmentation, congestion
and fine lines, and are tailored in strength to your skin and goals.

**What is Dermalux® LED light therapy?**
Dermalux® LED light therapy uses clinically proven wavelengths of light to calm
inflammation, target blemish-causing bacteria, and boost collagen. It is a relaxing,
non-invasive treatment that can be used on its own or to enhance results from other skin
treatments.

**Which skin treatment is right for my concern?**
We begin with a skin consultation to assess your skin and concerns, then build a
personalised plan that may combine treatments such as facials, peels, microneedling,
injectables and LED therapy for the best results.

**Do skin treatments involve downtime?**
Most of our skin treatments involve minimal downtime. Some — such as stronger peels or
microneedling — may cause temporary redness or flaking for a few days. Your practitioner
will explain exactly what to expect and provide tailored aftercare.
